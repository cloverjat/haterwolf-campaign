Scoring Formula

Base = (Likes × 1) + (Reposts × 3) + (Replies × 2) + (Views × 0.02)
Score = Base × Authenticity Multiplier × Relevance Multiplier

Higher weight on reposts & replies because they show real reach and discussion.

Anti-Bot / Authenticity Checks

  • • Engagement rate vs follower count (extremely high rates on small accounts = flag)

  • • Sudden spike patterns (when data available)

This is a heuristic system for the MVP. Real production can add deeper ML / third-party bot scores.
